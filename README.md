1. Project Goals
   - Compile and analyze the upset frequencies from a large dataset of
     real-world professional major league sports.
   - Create graph theoretical models of sports leagues and investigate the upset
     frequency in said models.
   - Compare and analyze the theoretical results with those of real world data
     of upset frequencies.
   - Analyze our results to draw overarching conclusions about predictability
     and competitiveness within major sports leagues.
